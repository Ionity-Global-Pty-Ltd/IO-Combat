Rules of the Grid

Best of 3 rounds, 99 seconds each. Standing block stops highs, mids and jump-ins; crouch block stops sweeps but not jump-ins. Grabs beat blocking. Specials chip through blocks. ION charges as you deal and take hits. When the deciding round is won, TERMINATE! appears: land a kick, UPLINK, special, blink or SURGE within 4 seconds to DELETE your rival. Elements: ION knockback · VOLT stun · CRYO freeze · VOID drains ION · SPORE poison · FIRE burn.
